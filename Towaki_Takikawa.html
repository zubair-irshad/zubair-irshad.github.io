I work on turning technical documents and diagrams into structured data. This helps companies in finance, manufacturing, and AEC build reliable AI agents that can understand visual information.

Until recently, I was a Research Scientist at NVIDIA Research working on 3D generative AI, geometry processing, and 3D computer vision. I have also done AI consulting work for AEC and entertainment.

I was also concurrently a Computer Science PhD student (on leave) at the University of Toronto, supervised by Sanja Fidler and Alec Jacobson. I have published over 10 research works with over 4000 citations (in top venues like CVPR, SIGGRAPH).
